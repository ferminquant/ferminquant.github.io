personal-mcp

A private, local tool that lets a personal AI assistant read, update, and format selected Google Sheets and read, create, update, and delete calendar events, including inviting attendees.

personal-mcp runs on the operator's own computer. It is not a hosted service and it does not provide accounts to the public. The Google integration is used only when the operator asks the assistant to read or update a spreadsheet, or to read or change a calendar event, including inviting attendees.

Google OAuth tokens are stored locally by the assistant on the operator's machine. The tool does not operate a server that receives or stores Google user data.

The tool is used by the operator and a small number of people known personally to them. It is not offered as a public service.

What it can access

  • Spreadsheet metadata and sheet names.
  • Cell values in ranges the operator explicitly requests.
  • Updating values or appending rows when the operator explicitly asks.
  • Setting display formats, colours, bold, and alignment.
  • Reading calendars and events.
  • Creating, updating, or deleting a calendar event when the operator explicitly asks.
  • Adding attendees and sending invitation or update emails when explicitly requested.
  • The tool does not expose calendar sharing, calendar creation, or calendar deletion.

Privacy
